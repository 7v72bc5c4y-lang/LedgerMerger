Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

The short version: your files stay in your browser. We never receive them.

What we do not collect

  • Your CSV files or anything inside them, such as transactions, names, amounts or account details.
  • Accounts, passwords, cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. The site uses none of them.

How your files are handled

Reading, merging and exporting happen inside your browser tab. The page tells your browser to block outside connections, and you can confirm that nothing is sent by watching the Network tab in your browser’s developer tools.

What we do receive

  • Hosting logs. Our web hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, pages requested and time of visit, to deliver and secure the site. Your files are never part of them.
  • Emails you send us. If you email us feedback or a bug report, we receive your email address and message. We use them to reply. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising. Please do not include real transaction data.
  • Tips and support. If we offer a way to leave a tip or support LedgerMerger, the payment is handled entirely by a third-party service with its own privacy policy. Depending on what you provide, we may see your name or email address and the amount.
  • GitHub issues. If you report a bug through GitHub, your report is public and GitHub’s own privacy policy applies.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete emails you have sent us. If you live in the EU, UK or California you may have additional rights over your personal information, and you can exercise them by contacting us.

Children

LedgerMerger is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
